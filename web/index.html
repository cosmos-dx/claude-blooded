questions

Good to know.

What do I actually get?

An MP4 in 9:16 (1080×1920) or 16:9 (1920×1080) with voice and music, a music-only version for your own voiceover, captions (.srt), the full script, and a posting pack with titles, captions, hashtags and the best time to post.

Can I control the voice and tone?

Yes. Describe the emotion and details you want, and add an optional voice prompt such as "deep, slow, lazy Bihari bhaiya" or "calm interviewer". The Voice Director casts from our voice library to match.

How long does a reel take?

Usually a few minutes for 30–60 seconds. Longer reels take longer to render. You can watch each stage as it happens.

Where are my files kept?

Videos are deleted from our servers 30 minutes after they're ready, so download them when they're done. Your script and posting pack stay in your account.

What if a video fails?

The credit goes straight back to your account.
